# Allowed version bumps: patch, minor, major
"@jahia/moonstone": minor

A screen reader now reads the label, the helper and the error of a `Field` on the `Input`, `Textarea` or `Dropdown` inside it, and the error is announced when it appears. `RadioGroup` and `CheckboxGroup` are named groups, `RadioItem` and `CheckboxItem` no longer need an `id`, and `FieldBoolean` and `Fieldset` link their label to their control (#1426)
